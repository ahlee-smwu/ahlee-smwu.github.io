> **Status:** Published, KMMS Autumn 2024 (first author).  
> **Role:** Principal Investigator on two supporting grants (WISET, Sookmyung SW).

## Research Objectives

- Multimodal emotion model built from dyadic (speaker–listener) datasets.
- Vision + audio integration for high-dimensional cognitive empathy.
- Multimodal alignment for temporally consistent responses.

## Motivation

Empathetic avatar systems were largely limited to simple "talking head" reactions and lacked higher-order cognitive empathy. Learning empathy correlations directly from dyadic interactions — with synchronized video and audio — offered a path beyond that limitation.

## Method

The pipeline integrates ViCo (video), AnyGPT (audio) and wav2lip / ESRGAN for alignment and enhancement, generating a listener response that stays synchronized with the speaker while reflecting the intended emotional reaction (*happy / worry / furious*).
